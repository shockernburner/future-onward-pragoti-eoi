Future Onward OPCCommercial Approach
Financial Proposal — Open Framework

Commercial terms follow technical definition

We deliberately keep pricing, lotting and financing structures open at this stage. Capex and opex are highly sensitive to fleet size, depot condition, grid capacity, charging strategy and localization depth — all of which require joint technical closure.

Open for detailed technical discussion: bus quantity & mix · charger power architecture · depot-by-depot works · spare-parts & warranty packages · training · phased rollout · localization with Pragoti · optional solar / storage · payment & financing structures.

Principle 1

Right-size infrastructure to validated BRTC duty cycles — no speculative overspend.

Principle 2

Transparent work packages so Pragoti and BRTC can compare scope on a like-for-like basis.

Principle 3

Commercial proposal issued after technical baseline workshop and site assessments.

Expression of Interest — not a closed bid price
