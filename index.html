Long-Term Industrial Pathway

KD and local assembly investment with Pragoti

KINWIN and partners are aligned on a phased Bangladesh industrial pathway with Pragoti: establish operational footprint now; scale to CKD/SKD assembly and plant investment as market demand grows.

PHASE 01

Footprint now

CBU / early supply through Pragoti for BRTC; aftersales setup; training; spare-parts pipeline; technical localization planning.

PHASE 02

KD ramp-up

Progressive CKD/SKD kits for local assembly — building on KINWIN’s proven KD supply experience (e.g. India) as volumes justify.

PHASE 03

Plant investment

When demand is established, invest in a dedicated assembly / KD plant in partnership with Pragoti and relevant authorities — jobs, technology transfer, industrial capability.

Our position: We are not seeking a one-off import transaction only. We seek a long-term industrial partnership anchored in Bangladesh.
Subject to market demand, policy support and joint feasibility with Pragoti
